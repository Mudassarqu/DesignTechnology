Criterion A: Empathize

Explains a persona; detailed storyboard from comprehensive task analysis; evaluates a range of products, accurately identifying successes/weaknesses of all key features.

Checklist: Persona analysis (~2pp) · Storyboard (~3pp, no extended writing) · Existing products (~4pp, 4–6 products: form, materials, ergonomics, manufacture, environment, safety)

A

Primary persona

Page 1
1234
Strand verdict. Persona analysis: clear, empathetic and relevant to the redesign. Needs evidence of where it came from.
1What went well

Complete persona: a 16-year-old school student with goals, frustrations, quotes and sliders for physical abilities and motivation.

2What went well

Specific problems with tables: too low to fit under a wheelchair, bending causes pain, equipment out of reach.

3What could be improved

The persona is written from general knowledge. Say how it was built (interview, observation) and who was observed.

4What could be improved

The ability sliders have no scale. Explain what each setting means for the design (for example limited reach).
