Criterion A: Empathize

Explains a persona; detailed storyboard from comprehensive task analysis; evaluates a range of products, accurately identifying successes/weaknesses of all key features.

Checklist: Persona analysis (~2pp) · Storyboard (~3pp, no extended writing) · Existing products (~4pp, 4–6 products: form, materials, ergonomics, manufacture, environment, safety)

Testing and feedback: three tests, three designs

Page 18
1234
Strand verdict. Evaluation: a numbered comparison with real feedback. Missing direct feedback from the user.
1What went well

Three tests: parents' focus group (6), teachers' questionnaire (6) and an expert interview.

2What went well

A table scores each design against user needs. Design 1 meets 11 needs, design 2 meets 9 and design 3 meets 5.
