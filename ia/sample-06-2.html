Criterion A: Empathize

Explains a persona; detailed storyboard from comprehensive task analysis; evaluates a range of products, accurately identifying successes/weaknesses of all key features.

Checklist: Persona analysis (~2pp) · Storyboard (~3pp, no extended writing) · Existing products (~4pp, 4–6 products: form, materials, ergonomics, manufacture, environment, safety)

A

Primary and secondary persona

Page 1
12345
Strand verdict. Persona analysis: detailed, empathetic and specific. Strongest part of Criterion A.
1What went well

Rich primary persona: a 38-year-old food seller carrying water about 2 km, with goals, limits and her own words about the container.

2What went well

The quotes name real problems: no grip under the container, flat bottom hurts the head, only a top handle.

3What went well

A secondary persona (14-year-old carer) widens the view of who uses the product and the distance and time involved.

4What could be improved

Show the persona or a similar user struggling with the real container. The photos shown are of people, not of the problem.

5What could be improved

The IA asks for one primary persona in depth. Use the second persona only to add what the first misses.
