Whole-IA requirements to check

  • 35 pages (at the limit); about 7,600 words extracted from the PDF including annotations and tables, so well over a 3,500-word reading is likely; a strict count would need checking
  • Several pages refer the reader to an appendix, which examiners will not read
  • In-text author names instead of page footnotes
  • Some annotations exceed 10 words
  • Physical fidelity model: met. Real user feedback: met. At least 3 ideas in C: met (5)

Criterion A: Empathize

Explains a persona; detailed storyboard from comprehensive task analysis; evaluates a range of products, accurately identifying successes/weaknesses of all key features.

Checklist: Persona analysis (~2pp) · Storyboard (~3pp, no extended writing) · Existing products (~4pp, 4–6 products: form, materials, ergonomics, manufacture, environment, safety)

A

Interview with the primary user

Page 1
12345
Strand verdict. Persona analysis: the stand-out strand. A real user with a real, specific need.
1What went well

Real, first-hand primary research: an interview with an elderly user (given as 80) who has lost fingers. This is exactly the evidence an "explains a persona" band needs.

2What went well

Photos of the user’s actual hand show the real issue with existing products (index and middle finger fully amputated). Checklist: "digital images of the persona".

3What went well

Opportunities and constraints are drawn out of the interview, so the persona sets a direction rather than just describing a person.

4What could be improved

Facts need pinning down early: age (80 here, 84 later) and how many fingers are affected must stay consistent across all pages.

52027 requirement

Page opens Criterion A. Footnote the interview date and consent on the page, not in an appendix.

Criterion B: Defining the Project

Problem statement explains key opportunities informed through the task analysis; specifications explain requirements with relevant and detailed reference to research.

Checklist: Problem statement (~1pp: situation, problem, research summary) · Product design specification (~3pp: essential + desirable, justified with sources)

Problem statement

Page 11
1234
Strand verdict. Problem statement: sound structure, but the link to the task analysis could be tighter.
1What went well

Summarises the task analysis and user need, then states the design outcome. The structure follows the checklist well.

2What could be improved

Relies on generic ageing statistics. Anchor the statement in what your own task analysis showed, in numbers if possible.

3What could be improved

"Design outcome" is vague ("prosthetic or device"). A clear, testable redesign opportunity earns the top band.

4What could be improved

The "requirements" line is just a list of tools; move it out and use the space for the research summary.
