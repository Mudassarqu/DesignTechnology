Whole-IA requirements to check

  • 31 pages: within the 35-page limit
  • About 4,200 words extracted from the PDF including annotations and tables, so close to or over 3,500 is possible; check a strict count
  • Citations are in-text, not page footnotes
  • Many annotations in Criterion C exceed 10 words
  • Physical model: met. User feedback: client interview and feedback on three ideas, but only one source of feedback visible. At least 3 ideas in C: met (5, 6, 9)

Criterion A: Empathize

Explains a persona; detailed storyboard from comprehensive task analysis; evaluates a range of products, accurately identifying successes/weaknesses of all key features.

Checklist: Persona analysis (~2pp) · Storyboard (~3pp, no extended writing) · Existing products (~4pp, 4–6 products: form, materials, ergonomics, manufacture, environment, safety)

A

Primary persona

Page 1
1234
Strand verdict. Persona analysis: clear and well organised, but not yet built on a real user observed with a real product.
1What went well

Clear persona card: age, work, family, goals, frustrations and a direct quote. Easy to see who the product is for.

2What went well

Frustrations are specific to the problem (no space for potting, no room to move plants, tools to store), so they feed straight into the brief.

3What could be improved

Persona images look like stock photos. The checklist asks for images of the persona showing issues with an existing product, so use your own user.

4What could be improved

Strength, creativity and knowledge bars are not explained or evidenced. Say how each was judged or leave them out.

Criterion B: Defining the Project

Problem statement explains key opportunities informed through the task analysis; specifications explain requirements with relevant and detailed reference to research.

Checklist: Problem statement (~1pp: situation, problem, research summary) · Product design specification (~3pp: essential + desirable, justified with sources)

Design opportunity

Starts from real data: urban density and the rise in apartment living, then links to balcony gardening.
