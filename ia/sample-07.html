Criterion A: Empathize

Explains a persona; detailed storyboard from comprehensive task analysis; evaluates a range of products, accurately identifying successes/weaknesses of all key features.

Checklist: Persona analysis (~2pp) · Storyboard (~3pp, no extended writing) · Existing products (~4pp, 4–6 products: form, materials, ergonomics, manufacture, environment, safety)

A

Personas from interviews

Page 1
1234
Strand verdict. Personas: realistic and based on interviews. They need quotes and a primary user.
1What went well

Two personas built from interviews: a lawyer working 12 hours a day and a banker who sits all day. Real daily routines and pain points.

2What went well

The banker wants to relieve aches whenever she is free but has no time for a massage. This is a clear need.

3What could be improved

Both personas are descriptions only. Add a short quote from each and mark which one is the primary user.

4What could be improved

Show each persona doing the task (sitting at the desk, trying to reach their back) rather than only describing it.
