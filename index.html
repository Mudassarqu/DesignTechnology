Who does your design leave out?

Every product is sized for someone. Drag the doorway and see who has to duck. Then learn why designers choose the 5th, 50th and 95th percentile.

Start with A1.1 Ergonomics
Doorway height 1900 mm
Illustrative adult stature: mean 1750 mm, standard deviation 75 mm. A doorway at the 95th percentile still makes 1 in 20 people duck. That is why safety-critical clearances use the 97.5th percentile or more.
