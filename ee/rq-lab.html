Research question lab

A good research question decides how good the essay can be. Practise spotting strong and weak questions, then test and build your own.

1. Narrow it down

Area: Sustainable packaging
Topic: Reusable bottles versus single-use bottles
Focus: Break-even number of uses for a stainless-steel bottle in the UAE
Question: To what extent is a reusable bottle more sustainable than a single-use bottle for students in Dubai, judged on life-cycle data and a usage survey?

Each step adds a product, a place, a user or a measure.

2. Spot the problem

3. Check your own draft

4. Build a question

Check before you commit

  • Can you collect evidence yourself, within about 40 hours?
  • Would two reasonable people reach different answers before the evidence?
  • Is it a design technology question and not a project to make a product?
  • Does it avoid your IA product, your IA data and your design project?
  • Have you shown it to your supervisor?
