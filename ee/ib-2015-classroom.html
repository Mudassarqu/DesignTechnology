Whole-essay points to check

  • Word count: the essay declares 3,466 words, which fits the 4,000 limit. Examiners stop reading at 4,000 and do not read appendices.
  • Research question: "How does the use of spatial design affect the atmosphere of a classroom?" is open and general. Try "To what extent do natural light and desk layout affect student-reported comfort in three types of classroom?"
  • Design cycle: the guide expects the design cycle to be used. This essay stops at analysis; there is no design recommendation or prototype.
  • Primary data: only short interviews. Add measurements (lux, temperature, noise, floor area per student) and a structured survey.
  • Sources: six written sources and three website photographs. Use a wider range, including standards and journal articles in design and ergonomics.

Criterion D: Presentation

Presentation: structure and layout that help the reader follow the argument. Check the contents page, headings, figures and references.

Criterion A: Focus and method

Focus and method: the topic, the research question and the methodology. A strong essay defines its terms, justifies its method, and keeps every section tied to the question.

2Focus and method · What could be improved

The abstract says "interviews with students ranged from year 10–12" but does not say how many students, or how "atmosphere" was judged. Methods are named but not justified.

Introduction

Essay page 4
1234
1Focus and method · What went well

Makes a personal link ("As a student in a secondary school…") and explains why classrooms matter. This gives the topic a purpose.

2Focus and method · What could be improved

Key terms are used loosely: "spatial design", "atmosphere", "mood" and "environment" seem to mean the same thing. Define atmosphere in a way you can measure (for example light level, noise, comfort ratings).

3Focus and method · What could be improved

The last sentence says the investigation will find "whether" spatial design can affect atmosphere. That is a yes/no question with an obvious answer. Aim for "to what extent" or "which layout" so the investigation can reach a specific conclusion.

4Focus and method · Check against the current rules

The research question must be set out prominently, justified, and be one a Design Technology investigation can answer. Here the RQ is general and sits closer to interior design than to the design cycle.
