IB essay 2013: a virtual DT workshop

A 2013 Design and Technology essay that builds a 3-D virtual workshop (SketchUp models in an Open Wonderland world) to help students, especially ESL students, revise tool safety. Read the student page on the left and the comments on the right. Numbered markers on the page edge match the numbered boxes.

This essay was originally assessed under the old A–K criteria. The comments here use the CURRENT criteria A–E (Focus and method, Knowledge and understanding, Critical thinking, Presentation, Engagement), the 2024 guide and its Design technology interpretation. Marks are not shown. Marker positions are approximate. Names are already redacted, and the cover, supervisor and examiner pages are not shown.

The essay is 3,975 words, close to the 4,000-word limit. It is a useful example of a project write-up that does many good DT things but is not yet a research essay.

Whole-essay points to check

  • Blue Word limit: 4,000 words. Examiners stop reading at the limit. This essay is just inside it at 3,975, so there was no room left for the missing analysis.
  • Blue Appendices: examiners do not read them. Here the survey results, interview evidence and measurement photos are in appendices or an external link, so the main text must stand alone.
  • Blue Double-dipping: the guide says the EE must not duplicate other work or reuse IA or project materials and data. A new project in the same software is fine; extending the same product is risky.
  • Blue An abstract is not one of the required elements in the current guide. Spend those words on analysis.

Criterion A: Focus and method

The topic is a real DT problem, but the question is too complex to be answered cleanly in 4,000 words.

Title page and research question

Essay page 1

1Focus and method · What could be improved

The question runs to about 50 words and combines four things: CAD models, a 3-D world, learning about tool safety, and ESL students. Pick one primary variable, e.g. "To what extent does a 3-D virtual workshop improve recall of safe machine use among ESL students in Grade 9 DT?"

2Focus and method · What could be improved

"To prove the potential of…" is a claim, not a question. Examiners expect an open question the evidence can answer either way.

3Presentation · Check against the current rules

The current guide wants the research question, subject and word count on the title page. Both are present here, which is good.

Criterion E: Engagement

No reflections form came with this sample, so Criterion E cannot be judged. In the essay itself there is real personal investment (five years of DT, building a school world, user trials) and some honest limits. The RPPF would need to go further and be evaluative: how did you change the question? What did you do when access was blocked? What would you do differently?
